# Attribution

Clean Slate is an unofficial tribute to **Digital Wipeout**, a Macintosh
shareware game by **John Mauro** (version 4.2, ©2000). It was distributed via Kagi
and described on the author's site at the time as
`http://www.johnmauro.com/software/`. Some of the board pictures bundled with the
original were contributed by **Fadeout Studios**.

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by John Mauro.

## What comes from the original, and what doesn't

**Taken from Digital Wipeout:** the game's *rules*:
- the 8×8 board of numbers
- three six-sided dice combined with the four basic operations
- scoring of one point plus one per wiped neighbour
- the illegal-move penalty
- the game ending after a run of passes in a row
- up to eight players

**Settled by the project owner** (the original's documentation doesn't spell
these out):
- the board's exact contents and order
- that all three dice must be used
- that division must come out whole

**Not taken:** any code, art, sound, board pictures, interface text or
documentation text. All of Clean Slate's code, visuals and wording are original.
The rules are described here in our own words.

**Added by Clean Slate:**
- shuffled boards with shareable codes
- the move aid
- the configurable pass limit
- showing an expression for each move
